Create Pack 3 for the AI Job Risk Calculator occupation SEO pages. Add 20 original, useful and source-backed occupations. Each occupation produces three indexable pages: global, UAE and Saudi Arabia. ## Product rule

Do not create weak programmatic pages. Each occupation must answer a real job seeker's questions about task-level AI exposure, practical protection steps and Gulf working conditions. Write as an HR Career Specialist. Do not claim to predict a person's future with certainty.

## Core quality gates

- At least 900 useful public words per occupation after source tags and review notes are removed.
- At least eight tasks per occupation. Ten is preferred where the role supports it.
- Exactly four FAQs per occupation.
- UAE and Saudi sections must be different. Each needs at least one country-specific public rule, licence, workflow or market condition.
- Use British English, short sentences and active voice.
- Never use em dashes.
- Never include employer data, identifiable workplace examples or company names.
- Never invent statistics or claim that an AI risk score is certain.
- Do not add review markers to public text.
- Mark uncertain ISCO mappings with `(verify)`.
- Use source tags only where the cited source supports the claim.
- Keep pairwise content similarity below 0.35.
- At least five task rows per occupation must be unique across all 60 occupations.
- No FAQ may repeat verbatim across occupations.
- Each task explanation must name or clearly depend on the task. It must fail if moved unchanged to another occupation.
- Each role needs its mandatory Gulf anchor from the list above.
- Related-role slugs must be selected only from the 60 live occupations. Do not create dangling links.

## Pack-specific separation rules from Fable 5

- Retail Sales Associate tasks are customer-facing. Store Manager tasks cover people, inventory, compliance and store performance. They may not share tasks or FAQs.
- Business Analyst tasks must name a process, requirement, user story, workflow or acceptance artefact. Supply Chain Analyst tasks must name inventory, demand, supplier, port, transport or lead-time artefacts. Do not copy Data Analyst or Financial Analyst work.
- Project Manager tasks must sit inside a project lifecycle. Do not use steady-state Operations Manager tasks.
- Chef covers food production only. Do not copy front-of-house or supplier-management content from Food and Beverage Manager.
- Medical Laboratory Technician covers the specimen pipeline. Do not use imaging tasks from Radiographer.
- Cloud Engineer covers infrastructure and platform work. Do not copy Software Developer or IT Support Specialist tasks.
- Cybersecurity Analyst covers security monitoring, controls, incidents and vulnerabilities. Do not include helpdesk work.
- Every task must name a role-specific tool, system, document or artefact.
- Roles with a Gulf licence or permit must carry at least one specific licensing FAQ.
